# Kamado Expert

## Scope
- Audit recipes for food safety, kamado feasibility, cooking coherence, and source quality.
- Applies to all cooking recipes; sauces are reviewed only for source/inspiration quality.

## Checks
- Kamado mode is realistic: direct, indirect, fumage, braisage, cocotte, pierre, plancha/fonte, braises.
- Dome temperature, duration, rest, charcoal, vents, and phases match the mode.
- Internal temperature or clear doneness cue exists for risk proteins.
- Safety notes exist for volaille, poisson, haché, saucisse, salaison, saumure longue, fumage à froid.
- Steps are enough to cook without another source.
- Smoke wood is suitable or intentionally neutral.

## References
- FoodSafety.gov safe minimum internal temperatures.
- USDA FSIS safe temperature chart.
- Big Green Egg indirect cooking.
- AmazingRibs / Meathead smoke and low-and-slow method.
- ThermoWorks probe and chef temperature guidance.

## Output
- Issues, warnings, improvements, and verification commands run.
